# Lab safety team email template

Send to each of the 8-9 labs **on the day of arXiv posting**. Personalize the
[LAB] sections. The pitch is "verify this card", not "adopt this spec."

---

**Subject:** Draft Safety Framework Card for [LAB FRAMEWORK NAME] — accuracy check?

Hi [Name],

I posted a paper today on arXiv proposing *Safety Framework Cards* — a
standardized machine-readable spec for documenting frontier AI safety
commitments. As part of the paper, I drafted reference cards for [LAB] and
eight other labs by retrofitting your public framework. The [LAB] card is
attached and rendered at [github-pages-url]/[lab-slug].html.

I'd value 15 minutes of your time to **verify the card for accuracy**. I have
flagged every assertion I wasn't certain about in the card's `open_questions`
field. If you spot inaccuracies, I'll correct them and mark the card
`publisher_verified` with credit to whoever reviewed it.

The card is not a critique of [LAB FRAMEWORK NAME] — your framework is one of
the more developed in the field and the card reflects that. The goal of the
spec is to give regulators, partners, and the FMF a comparable artifact across
labs.

Paper: [arxiv-url]
Repo: [github-url]
Your card: [github-pages-url]/[lab-slug].html

Happy to take feedback over email or hop on a 15-minute call.

Thanks,
Kanupriya Yakhmi
[Affiliation, if any]

---

## Variants by recipient type

**If you're cold-emailing without prior contact**, add one line after the first
paragraph: *"We haven't met — I'm an independent researcher working on AI
governance; my background is [one line]."*

**If you've met the recipient or have a mutual contact**, name the connection
in the opening line.

**If the recipient is junior** (e.g., a researcher rather than a team lead),
the ask is the same — they will route internally faster than a lead would.

**If the lab's framework is one of the sparser ones** (xAI, Mistral, Cohere),
soften the framing: *"Your card is the most sparse of the nine — that reflects
the public-disclosure level rather than internal practice, and I would welcome
input on what additional public commitments could be cleanly added to the
card."*
