Capability Threshold (Cyber): Capability to materially uplift sophisticated cyber operations.
Capability Threshold (AI R&D): Capability to autonomously accelerate frontier AI research and development.
ASL-3 Safeguards: Group of technical and operational safeguards activated for models reaching capability thresholds. Activated May 2025.
ASL-4 Safeguards: Group of safeguards for higher capability levels; definitions intentionally left to be developed as capabilities approach.
